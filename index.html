Our Mission

Empowering Through Education

At Kashipur Tarun Projontho, we believe in the power of education to transform lives. Our mission is to ensure that every child in our community has access to quality education, regardless of their financial circumstances.

We provide:

  • Educational support for underprivileged students
  • Financial assistance for education
  • Community development initiatives
  • Environmental conservation projects
